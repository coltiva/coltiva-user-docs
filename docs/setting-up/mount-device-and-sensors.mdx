# Mount the device and sensors

Do this before you fill the reservoir: the water level sensor goes under the tank.

1. **Glue the water level sensor to the underside of the reservoir.** Choose a flat spot that stays straight when the tank is full. Which glue to use and how to place it: [Water level sensor](../sensors/level.md).

2. **Choose a place for the control unit.** It must stay dry, out of direct sunlight, and within cable reach of the sensors and the supply tanks. Fix the bracket with screws or double-sided tape ([prepare the surface](./preparing-surfaces-for-gluing-or-taping.mdx) first), then slide the unit onto it.

3. **Put the pH, EC and water temperature sensors in the reservoir.** Clip them to the sensor platform and fix the platform to the tank with its magnets or suction cups. The probes must stay under water at all times.

4. **Connect the sensors to the control unit.** The pH sensor uses the round BNC connector. Do not touch its centre pin: the circuit behind it is sensitive to static electricity. See [pH](../sensors/pH.md#esd-protection-of-the-bnc-connector).

5. **Plug in the power supply.** Use the supplied 12 V adapter, or one that meets [Specifications](../specifications.md#power-supply). The logo lights up bright, with a short dark pause every few seconds: the device is waiting for its Wi-Fi settings.

The air temperature and humidity sensor is built into the underside of the control unit, so there is nothing to mount for it.
