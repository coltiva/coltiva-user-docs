# Install the pumps and tanks

The control unit has seven numbered pump outlets plus one marked **circ pump**. Which pump goes where:

| Purpose | Pump | Where it sits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dose nutrients, pH adjusters or additives | Dosing pump (peristaltic) | Outside the liquid, with its intake hose in the supply bottle |
| Refill with fresh water | Volume pump (centrifugal) | On the bottom of the fresh-water tank |
| Drain the reservoir | Volume pump (centrifugal) | On the bottom of the reservoir |
| Circulate the nutrient solution | Volume pump (centrifugal), on the **circ pump** outlet | In the reservoir |

1. **Mount each pump** with its magnets or suction cups. See [Mounting](../pumps.mdx#mounting).
2. **Route the hoses so that nothing can siphon.** A hose end that lies below the liquid level of the tank it draws from keeps flowing after the pump has stopped. Read [Avoiding siphoning](../pumps.mdx#avoiding-siphoning) before you cut any hose.
3. **Connect the pumps to the outlets** and write down which outlet each pump is on. You pick pumps by outlet number in the app.
