# Calibrate the pumps and sensors

Calibration is what makes doses and readings accurate. Do it once now. The [Maintenance](../maintenance.md) page says when to repeat it.

| What | Why | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Every pump** | The device doses by running a pump for a calculated time, so it must know each pump's flow. Uncalibrated pumps cannot dose at all. | [Calibrating a pump](../pumps.mdx#calibrating-a-pump) |
| **pH sensor** | A new probe needs its first calibration, and pH probes drift over time. | [pH calibration](../sensors/pH.md#calibration) |
| **EC sensor** | Calibrate before you rely on EC readings or EC Control. | [EC calibration](../sensors/EC.md#calibration) |

That completes the setup. Where to go next:

- [Using the app](../using-the-app.md): readings, alarms and running pumps by hand.
- [Automation](../automation/how-it-works.md): let Coltiva hold pH, EC and water level for you.
