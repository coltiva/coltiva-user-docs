# Connect the device to the app

The Coltiva device talks to the app through the cloud, over your Wi-Fi. Connecting it takes a few minutes and is done entirely from your phone.

## What you need

- The Coltiva app, from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=eu.coltiva.coltivaapp).
- A **2.4 GHz** Wi-Fi network and its password. The device cannot join 5 GHz networks.
- The QR code on the device.
- The device powered on, within good Wi-Fi range of your router.

## Steps

1. **Create an account** in the app, or log in.
2. Tap **Set up a new Coltiva device**. If this is your first device, the app first asks you to name your *group*: the shared account for everyone who looks after the same grow systems. Name it after your grow room or company. Later devices are added with **Set up or reconfigure a device** at the bottom of the main screen.
3. **Check that the device is in setup mode.** The illuminated logo is bright, with a clear dark pause every few seconds. A steady glow means the device is already set up. See [Reset the device](../troubleshooting/reset-the-device.md) to put it back into setup mode.
4. Tap **Scan QR code** and point the camera at the QR code on the device.
5. The app connects your phone directly to the device, which then looks for Wi-Fi networks. **Choose your network**, enter its password and tap **Provision**.
6. Wait while the device joins your Wi-Fi and is linked to your account. The app confirms with *Done! Your Coltiva joined …*.

The device starts measuring right away. Open your grow system: the first readings usually appear within 30 seconds. If not, pull down to refresh.

## If the password was not accepted

The app tells you, and the logo shows a short flash once a second for about 10 seconds before it returns to the setup-mode pattern. Tap **Try again** and enter the password once more. No reset is needed.

## Changing to a new Wi-Fi network

The device never goes back into setup mode on its own. To move it to another network, or after your network's name or password has changed, [reset the device](../troubleshooting/reset-the-device.md) and connect it again. Your calibrations and automations are kept.
