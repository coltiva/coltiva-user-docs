# Overview

Coltiva watches your hydroponic reservoir and keeps it in range. The control unit reads six sensors (pH, EC, water level, water temperature, air temperature and humidity), drives up to eight pumps, and connects over Wi-Fi to the Coltiva app on your phone. From the app you follow the readings, get alarms, run pumps by hand, and set up automation that holds pH, EC and water level on its own.
