# Taking the system down

Do this whenever the system will be out of use for more than a few days: between crops, before a move, or to put it into storage. Nutrient or pH adjuster left to dry inside a pump attacks its tubing, and a pH probe that dries out is ruined for good. The whole procedure takes about half an hour.

## Before you start

1. **Switch every automation off** in the app. A probe lifted out of the water, or a pump being flushed, would otherwise start a correction and leave the automation in *Needs attention*.
2. **Leave the power connected** until the pumps have been flushed. You need the app to run them.
3. **Close the supply bottles.** Take each dosing pump's intake hose out of its bottle and put the cap back on.

## Pumps

Do this for every pump, dosing (peristaltic) and volume (centrifugal) alike, including the circulation pump.

1. **Flush with warm water.** Put the intake hose of a dosing pump, or a volume pump itself, in a container of warm tap water, around 40 °C and not hotter, and lead the outlet hose to a bucket or a drain. [Run the pump by hand](./pumps.mdx#running-a-pump-by-hand): 100 ml two or three times for a dosing pump, a litre or two for a volume pump. This rinses the nutrient, pH adjuster or other liquid out of the pump and all of its hoses before it can dry in.
2. **Empty the hoses.** Lift the intake out of the water and run the same amount again, so that the pump pushes the water out of the hoses. A volume pump drains by itself when you lift it out. Do not run it dry for more than a moment.
3. **Dosing pumps: empty the tube in the pump head.** Pull both hoses off the pump and run it by hand for 5 ml. Some water always stays in the tube inside the pump head, and this short run pushes it out. A few seconds without liquid does no harm. Do not run the pump dry for longer than that.
4. **Dry the hoses** that you are going to use again. Blow them through with compressed air, from a compressor or a can of compressed air. Wipe the pumps dry on the outside. Hoses that are put away damp grow mould inside.
5. **Dosing pumps: take the squeeze off the tube.** If the pump will be stored for more than a few weeks, twist the transparent cover of the pump head anticlockwise until it pops off, and lift the tube off the rollers. Keep the cover with the pump. A tube that is left squeezed flat between the rollers for months takes a permanent set, and the pump doses too little afterwards.
6. **Store the pumps** dry, cool and out of direct sunlight, together with their hoses. The limits under [Environmental protection](./specifications.md#environmental-protection) apply in storage too.

## Sensors

1. **Lift the pH, EC and water temperature probes out** and rinse them in clean water. If residue is left, clean each probe as described on its page under [Sensors](/docs/category/sensors). Never wipe the pH probe's glass.
2. **Put the pH probe in storage solution straight away.** Fill its cap with storage solution and push the cap on. The glass must never dry out. Which solution to use, and what to do with a probe that has dried out, is on [pH](./sensors/pH.md#storage).
3. **Dry the EC and water temperature sensors** and keep them with the control unit.
4. **Leave the water level sensor on the tank.** It is glued on and would have to be glued again. Store the tank so that nothing rests on the sensor or its cable.

## Control unit

1. **Disconnect the power**, then unplug the pumps and the sensors. Do not touch the centre pin of the pH sensor's BNC connector: see [ESD protection](./sensors/pH.md#esd-protection-of-the-bnc-connector).
2. **Keep it dry** and within the limits under [Environmental protection](./specifications.md#environmental-protection). There is no need to reset the device. It keeps its Wi-Fi settings, calibrations and automations, and reconnects on its own when it gets power again. Reset it only if it is going to join a different network: see [Reset the device](./troubleshooting/reset-the-device.md).

## Setting the system up again

Mount and connect everything as described under [Setting up](/docs/category/setting-up). Before you rely on the system again:

- **pH probe:** if the solution in the cap has dried out, soak the probe in storage solution for 24 hours first. Calibrate it, and replace it if the app reports it as worn.
- **EC sensor:** calibrate it.
- **Dosing pumps:** look at the tube before you put the cover back on and fit the hoses. Replace it if it has stayed flat, or is hard, sticky, cracked or discoloured. A tube that was flushed, dried and freed from the rollers is normally fine to use again.
- **Every pump:** calibrate it again, whether or not you changed the tube. Tubing changes with age, so the old calibration no longer holds. See [Calibrating a pump](./pumps.mdx#calibrating-a-pump).
- **Automation:** switch it back on once the probes are under water and calibrated.
