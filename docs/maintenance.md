# Maintenance

| How often | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Every 3 months**, and whenever you replace the probe | Calibrate the pH sensor. The app reports the probe's health afterwards; replace a probe it reports as worn. See [pH](./sensors/pH.md#calibration). |
| **When EC readings look off** | Clean the EC sensor's metal tips and calibrate it. See [EC](./sensors/EC.md). |
| **After changing a pump's tubing** | Recalibrate that pump. See [Calibrating a pump](./pumps.mdx#calibrating-a-pump). |
| **Regularly** | Inspect pumps, tubing and cables for wear, kinks and leaks. Check that hose ends still sit where they should, so nothing can siphon. |
| **Before a supply bottle runs dry** | Top it up. An empty bottle is the most common reason for an automation to stop with *Needs attention*. |

## Cleaning

- **pH probe:** rinse in probe storage or cleaning solution, or in pH 4 buffer. Never wipe the glass electrode. See [pH](./sensors/pH.md#cleaning).
- **EC and water temperature sensors:** mild detergent, or an isopropyl alcohol based disinfectant. Stubborn residue on the EC tips comes off with a scouring pad.
- **Air temperature and humidity sensor:** do not clean it.
- **Control unit:** keep it dry.

## Software updates

The device updates its own software over Wi-Fi and restarts afterwards. Nothing is needed from you. The app is updated through the app store, and tells you if your version is too old to keep working.
